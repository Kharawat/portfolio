Passion projectF1 · Aero & 3D

RB19 CFD Lab

Pure F1 obsession, built for fun. I took a public RB19 model and rebuilt the aero parts from race-weekend photos: a four-element front wing, the rear and beam wings, and a continuous underfloor with a stepped diffuser. The new underbody passes OpenFOAM surface checks.

Working DRS. The rear flap rotates about 18.5° on one fixed hinge, driven by a single 0–1 control in Blender. Checked at 16 positions: no clashes with the fixed wing, and the slot gap opens from 17–22 mm to 66–82 mm.

Next. Whole-car surface repair, a fresh volume mesh, then a 50 m/s moving-ground run. No aerodynamic numbers yet.

  • Blender
  • OpenFOAM
  • Python
  • Mesh QA
